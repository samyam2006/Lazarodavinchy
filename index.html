The House of Lazaro Davinchy

Where Craft Meets Art

Named in the spirit of the great masters, Lazaro Davinchy treats every commission like a canvas. We source certified stones, cast in solid gold, and set every diamond by hand, no shortcuts, no compromises.

Whether it's your engagement ring, your first timepiece, or a pendant nobody else will ever own, we build it with you from sketch to final polish.
