Our Story

No Shortcuts.
No Compromises.

Lazaro Davinchy was built on a simple idea: jewelry should be made the way art is made — by hand, with obsession over every detail. We source certified stones, cast in solid gold, and set every diamond ourselves.

Whether it's your engagement ring, your first timepiece, or a pendant nobody else will ever own, we build it with you from sketch to final polish — and send you videos every step of the way.

  • Certified natural & lab-grown diamonds
  • Solid 10k, 14k & 18k gold — never plated
  • In-house design, casting & stone setting
  • Lifetime warranty on every setting we craft
